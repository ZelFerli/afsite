Strategic Recommendations

Growth & Diversification Roadmap

Six priorities for 2025, ranked by urgency.

01
Diversify Revenue Away from Acqua di Parma

A single heritage brand generates ~20% of total revenue from just 30 SKUs. Build depth in high-performing New brands — Dedcool, Nishane, and Mancera all show strong ratings and growing volume. Invest now before valuations rise.

02
Fix Inventory Immediately

49 products with zero sales represent a capital liability and ranging clutter. Audit all of them: promote high-rated hidden gems through editorial or sample programmes, and liquidate genuinely stagnant stock. Set a standing stock-cover threshold of 20× annual sales to prevent recurrence.

03
Monitor High-Revenue, Low-Satisfaction Products

Le Rose (Creed) and Secret Royale (Skylar) both rank in the top 10 by revenue despite 3.9 ratings. Novelty-driven sales fade once word-of-mouth catches up — invest in satisfaction improvements or diversify reliance away from these products before churn materialises.

04
Rationalise the Oriental Family

138 Oriental SKUs — the most of any family — rank only 4th in revenue. The long tail of low-velocity products is diluting buyer focus and ranging efficiency. A targeted cull would sharpen the range and improve overall sell-through.

05
Expand Physical Retail for Online-Heavy Brands

Brands with over 65% online exposure are missing in-store discovery — particularly important in fragrance, where trial drives conversion. Explore targeted wholesale or pop-up partnerships to broaden physical reach without heavy infrastructure investment.

06
Launch an Oriental/Amber Discovery Set for Retail

Oriental/Amber holds the portfolio's highest average rating (4.53) and is 69% online — proven quality with limited physical presence. A curated retail discovery set would bridge DTC success with in-store trial and introduce the Colonia range to a new audience who may not shop online.
